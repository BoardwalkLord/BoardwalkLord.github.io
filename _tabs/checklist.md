This is a free, 30-minute self-assessment for Malaysian businesses that supply goods or services to other organisations. When a customer is subject to the Cyber Security Act 2024 — or is simply cautious — its compliance duties reach you through your contract: data-protection clauses, breach-notification deadlines, audit rights, certification warranties. This checklist helps you find those obligations in your own agreement and, more importantly, test whether you could actually *prove* you meet them if the customer asked.

You work through it privately, with your contract in front of you. Your answers stay with you — nothing you tick is submitted anywhere.
